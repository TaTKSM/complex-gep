# Widely Linear Gaussian Equivalence for Complex Random Features
arXiv

# Author
Takuya Kanazawa (Kobe Gakuin University)

# Abstract
Entrywise nonlinearity makes the spectral analysis of high-dimensional random feature matrices substantially more difficult than that of the underlying matrix products. We establish a spectral Gaussian equivalence for complex random feature matrices with general, possibly non-holomorphic activation functions. The widely linear surrogate combines the preactivation matrix and its complex conjugate with independent Gaussian residual noise that may be improper. Under a condition ensuring proper preactivations, we prove convergence of the expected empirical spectral distributions of the nonlinear feature covariance matrix and its surrogate to a common limit in the proportional high-dimensional regime. The proof combines a complex Hermite expansion with a graphical moment method. We also characterize broad classes of activation functions for which one or both response channels vanish. Numerical experiments confirm the predicted spectral equivalence.
